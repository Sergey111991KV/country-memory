## Full description

**Flagfield** helps you learn countries through three game modes:

🌍 **Globe Quest** — Find countries on a spinning 3D political globe. Select, verify, and learn with capital, continent, and flag.

🗺️ **Map Quest** — Same challenge on a flat political world map.

🚩 **Flag Quiz** — Recognize countries from large animated flags. Four answers per round; smart distractors.

✨ **Country Portrait** — Long-press a flag for a full-screen cultural scene.

📈 **Progress** — Track mastery by country and mode.

🎯 **Daily goals** — Short sessions that fit your day.

**Free tier** includes multiple completed game sessions. **Premium** unlocks unlimited play via subscription (and optional lifetime if offered).

• ~195 countries  
• UI in English, Russian, and more  
• No account required  
• Kid-friendly (parental guidance for purchases)
