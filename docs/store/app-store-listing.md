## Description

**Learn the world — one country at a time.**

Flagfield turns geography into short, satisfying sessions you can play anywhere.

**Globe Quest**  
Find countries on a beautiful 3D political globe. Tap to select, verify, and learn — capital, continent, and animated flag.

**Map Quest**  
The same challenge on a flat political world map — perfect if you prefer a classic atlas view.

**Flag Quiz**  
Large, lively flags and four smart answers. Ten rounds per session build real recognition.

**Country Portrait**  
Long-press any flag for a full-screen cultural moment (video when available).

**Progress & daily goals**  
Track what you’ve practiced. Short daily targets keep you motivated.

**Free to start**  
Generous free sessions. Premium unlocks unlimited play in all modes.

No account required. Core data loads once; flags may need network.

---

**Premium**  
Auto-renewing subscription and/or lifetime option when offered. Payment charged to Apple ID. Manage in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
